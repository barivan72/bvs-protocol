A few good questions.

Are these live prices?

No. UK listings use GBP reference figures; Sony aibo and EMO use clearly labelled USD references. Sources were reviewed on 9 September 2026. Some underlying pages are cached snapshots. Prices can change or differ by model, colour, bundle or promotion. Subscriptions, consumables, shipping and extras may cost more. The seller’s checkout is the final source.

Who takes my order and sends my purchase?

The seller linked from each product. Pet Gadget Club is a discovery catalogue, not a retailer or dropshipping checkout. We do not hold stock, accept payments or manage delivery and returns.

Have you tested these products?

No. This is a source-based collection, not a hands-on review lab. We do not invent star ratings or customer reviews. The catalogue contains the original 50 pet-tech listings plus eight robot listings. Three robot entries are specialist configurations of the Go2 EDU Plus. The pet-tech collection includes three retailer bundles and three seller-refurbished trackers; stock is not monitored live.

Will the links earn affiliate commission?

This release uses direct retailer links. Affiliate tracking has not been connected and commission income has not been verified. Any future affiliate or paid links must be clearly identified.

Where are my saved products kept?

Only in this browser’s local storage. There is no account or cross-device sync. Export your shortlist as a text file to keep a copy or share it. Clearing browser data or using private browsing can remove the list.
